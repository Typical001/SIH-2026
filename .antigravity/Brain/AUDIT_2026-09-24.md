## Follow-up implementation: iceberg rendering

The previous per-iceberg animated marker/popup/buffer/trail render was replaced by `OfficialIcebergLayer.jsx`. It uses `icebergVisibility.js` for finite-coordinate and great-circle filtering, draws a maximum of 150 visible dots through one canvas renderer, defaults to a route corridor, and offers zoom-gated area exploration. A selected dot alone receives detailed popup, forecast, buffer and trajectory rendering. App now joins compact and detailed forecast responses. Focused tests passed 13/13 and the production build passed. The full catalog is still fetched; NAV-50 tracks server-side filtering/pagination/vector tiles.
